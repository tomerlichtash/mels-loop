Gerald Jay Sussman is an MIT professor of electrical engineering and computer science. He co-created the Scheme programming language with Guy Steele and co-authored *Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs* (SICP), one of the most influential CS textbooks. He has been at MIT's AI Lab since the late 1960s.
