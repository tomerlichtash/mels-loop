Marvin Minsky (1927–2016) was an American cognitive and computer scientist who co-founded MIT's AI Laboratory. A pioneer of artificial intelligence, he made fundamental contributions to neural networks, computer graphics, and the philosophy of mind. His book *The Society of Mind* proposed that intelligence emerges from the interaction of simple agents.
