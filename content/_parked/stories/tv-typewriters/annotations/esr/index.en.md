Eric S. Raymond (ESR) is an American software developer and open-source advocate. He maintained the Jargon File, a compendium of hacker slang and folklore, and authored *The Cathedral and the Bazaar*, an influential essay on open-source development. Many of the editorial notes in the Jargon File's story entries are his.
