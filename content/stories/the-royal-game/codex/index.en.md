---
title: "The Royal Game"
subtitle: "Originally published in 1941 by Stefan Zweig"
---

On the great liner which was to leave New York for Buenos Aires at midnight, there was the usual last-minute bustle and commotion. Visitors from shore pushed confusedly through the passages, friends chatted while standing cheek to cheek with farewell drinks in hand, all the while telegraph boys in tilted caps threaded their way through groups of people, loudspeakers ceaselessly summoning passengers.

I was standing on the promenade deck watching the last moorings that still tied us to the pier being cast off, when somebody beside me spoke my name. I looked up. It was one of my older acquaintances.

"A pleasant crossing," he said, and smiled. "A pleasant crossing," I echoed.

But that was all we found to say. Sometimes, between old acquaintances, silence is more comfortable than words. We stood together watching the water darken between the ship and the shore.
