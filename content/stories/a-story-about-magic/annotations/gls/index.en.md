Guy L. Steele Jr. (GLS) is an American computer scientist who has contributed to the design of Scheme, Common Lisp, and Fortress. He co-authored the Jargon File with Eric S. Raymond and is known for his work at MIT's AI Lab, Sun Microsystems, and Oracle.
